Vostro Content Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Vostro Content is a creator workspace used to prepare, preview, and publish short-form videos to connected social accounts after creator confirmation.

Information we process

When a creator connects TikTok, the service may process basic TikTok profile information such as account identifiers, display name, and avatar, as well as OAuth access tokens, posting permissions, post metadata, captions, media files, selected privacy settings, commercial-content disclosure settings, publish identifiers, and publishing status.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the connected account, show the creator identity in the posting screen, prepare video posts, send approved media to TikTok through the Content Posting API, fetch publishing status, and keep a record of creator-confirmed publishing actions.

Storage

Access tokens and related credentials are stored in protected application configuration and are not intentionally published. Content records and publishing records may be stored by the service for workflow tracking and status review.

Sharing

Information is shared with TikTok only as needed to authenticate, publish creator-approved videos, upload media, and retrieve publishing status. We do not sell personal information.

Data deletion

Creators can revoke TikTok access in their TikTok account settings. Creators may also request deletion of stored connection records, content records, and publishing records by contacting Vostro Content.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Vostro Content through the connected social profile or support channel provided in the application.
