Vostro Content Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Vostro Content is a workflow tool used to prepare, review, and publish short-form AI workflow content for the Vostro Content blog and related social channels.

Information we process

When connected to a third-party platform, the service may process basic account identifiers, profile information, publishing permissions, post metadata, captions, media files, and publishing or analytics results needed for the workflow.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate platform access, prepare content, publish approved posts, fetch publishing status, and review basic performance data.

Storage

Access tokens and related credentials are stored locally by the operator and are not intentionally published. Content drafts and publishing records may be stored in local project files for workflow tracking.

Sharing

Information is shared with third-party platforms only as needed to authenticate, upload, publish, or retrieve content and analytics. We do not sell personal information.

Data deletion

If access is no longer needed, platform tokens can be revoked in the relevant platform settings and removed from local storage.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of the Vostro Content blog through the connected social profile.
