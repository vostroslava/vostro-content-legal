Vostro Content

Vostro Content is a creator workspace for preparing, previewing, and publishing short-form videos to connected social accounts.

Creators can connect their own TikTok account with Login Kit, review a video preview and caption, choose TikTok posting settings, confirm commercial-content disclosures, and publish approved videos through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Vostro Content only sends media to TikTok after the connected creator confirms the publishing action.
